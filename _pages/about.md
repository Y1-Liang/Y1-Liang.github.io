<p class="lead">
  Hi, I'm <strong>Liang Yi (易亮)</strong>, an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at the
  <a href="https://www.ustc.edu.cn/">University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)</a>.
  My interests lie at the intersection of <strong>trustworthy AI, multimodal learning, and 3D spatial intelligence</strong>.
  I conduct undergraduate research in the <a href="https://data-science.ustc.edu.cn/">Lab of Data Science (LDS)</a> at USTC.
</p>

## Research Interest

I am broadly interested in building reliable multimodal systems that can perceive and reason about the world. My current research focuses on reducing hallucinations in multimodal large language models, improving robust preference alignment, and developing stronger spatial understanding for 3D and embodied intelligence.
