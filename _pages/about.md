## Research Experience

### Undergraduate Researcher · LDS Lab, USTC
*2024 – Present*

I work on reliable multimodal AI systems, including hallucination mitigation and robust preference alignment for MLLMs, as well as 3D multimodal perception and spatial understanding.

Current directions include:

- **Reliable multimodal alignment:** improving robustness to noisy or unreliable preference supervision.
- **3D spatial intelligence:** point-cloud adaptation, spatial reasoning, grounding, and fine-grained 3D understanding.
- **Multimodal model engineering:** large-scale training and evaluation with PyTorch, Transformers, DeepSpeed, and multi-GPU systems.
